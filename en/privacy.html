2.1. We collect data that you voluntarily leave in the forms on the website: Name, Phone, Telegram, Email, company name, as well as the history of communication with the AI manager.

4.1. We do not sell or transfer your personal data to third parties, except as provided by law, as well as technical partners (for example, Telegram, Cloudflare) that ensure the operation of the service.

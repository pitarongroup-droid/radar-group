How the AI Manager trains for your product

Seven examples. One mechanism: we study the product, audience and objections, then train the bot — and it sells like the best manager.

What we studied: car models, typical works, price ranges, timelines, warranty on works and parts.
Client:hi, how much is an oil change on a Camry?
AI:Hello! For a 2019 Camry — $120 with labor and filter, if the oil is ours. $40 if you bring your own. What year is your car and what oil do you use? 🙂
Client:2019, I bring my own oil
AI:Then $40 for labor. Warranty on the work — 6 months. When would you like to come in? We have slots tomorrow at 10:00 and 14:00. 🙂
Client:tomorrow at 10
AI:Booked for tomorrow at 10:00. Please leave your name and phone — I'll confirm the appointment. 🙂
Result: the bot quoted the price, handled "I bring my own oil", offered slots and closed the booking.
What we studied: course programs, formats, duration, prices, installments, alumni reviews.
Client:want to learn Python, how much is it?
AI:The course is 8 weeks, $300 or 4 payments of $80. The first trial lesson is free. Are you starting from zero or already coding? 🙂
Client:from zero, but $300 is too much
AI:I hear you! That's why there's an installment plan of $80 — start without a big payment. And the first lesson is free: see the format, then decide. Shall I sign you up for the trial? 🙂
Client:sure
AI:Great! Leave your name and Telegram — I'll send the lesson link and schedule. 🙂
Result: "too expensive" handled through installments and a free trial, not discounts.
What we studied: treatment directions, price ranges, duration, warranty, payment plans, doctors' specializations, booking rules.
Client:how much is teeth cleaning?
AI:Professional cleaning is $70 and takes about 45 minutes, including ultrasound and polishing. If there's tartar, the doctor confirms the exact plan at the appointment. When was your last cleaning? 🙂
Client:a year ago, but I'm afraid to go
AI:I understand, that's very common. We have sedation and a doctor who works with anxious patients. You can start with a consultation — 15 minutes, no treatment. Shall I book it? 🙂
Client:yes, please
AI:Great! Leave your name and phone — I'll pick the right doctor and a convenient time. 🙂
Result: the bot eased the fear, offered a soft entry via consultation and closed the appointment.
What we studied: practice areas, consultation format, fees, timelines, required documents, confidentiality rules.
Client:need to challenge a traffic fine
AI:We handle traffic fines. The initial review is $30 and is included in the fee if we take the case. Please send a photo of the resolution and the date you received it. Have you appealed within 10 days? 🙂
Client:got it 3 days ago, sending the photo now
AI:Great, the deadline is not missed. The review takes 1–2 days; if successful, we'll represent you in court. Let's set up a call to discuss the details. Is today after 15:00 convenient? 🙂
Client:yes, that works
AI:Booked for 15:30. Please leave your name and phone — the lawyer will call you at the scheduled time. 🙂
Result: the bot checked the deadline, explained the process and booked a call without giving legal advice.
What we studied: service menu, price list, duration, masters, contraindications, aftercare, booking slots.
Client:how much is balayage?
AI:Balayage starts at $120 depending on hair length and density; with toner and care it's up to $180. It takes 3–4 hours. What's your current color and length? 🙂
Client:dark, shoulder-length. bit pricey
AI:For your length it'll be closer to $120–140. Plus aftercare — the color lasts much longer. Shall I book a free consultation with a colorist to get an exact quote? 🙂
Client:yes
AI:Great! Leave your name and phone — the colorist will reach out and pick a time. 🙂
Result: the bot clarified parameters, upsold aftercare and handled "too pricey" via consultation.
What we studied: estimate ranges, work stages, timelines, warranty, materials and what affects the final price.
Client:how much to renovate a 2-room apartment?
AI:Turnkey renovation starts at $250/m² for cosmetic work and $450/m² with engineering. For a 55 m² flat that's roughly $13,750–$24,750. Do you have a design project or need measurements? 🙂
Client:no project, 60 sqm
AI:For 60 m² turnkey it's around $15,000–$27,000; more precise after measurement. Measurement and estimate are free. When can our surveyor visit? 🙂
Client:Saturday afternoon
AI:Saturday afternoon — perfect. Leave your name, phone and address — I'll confirm the time with the surveyor. 🙂
Result: the bot calculated the range by area, explained the price factors and booked a free measurement.
What we studied: cargo types, tariffs (hourly / fixed), floors, elevator availability, furniture disassembly, packing, timelines, and movers' work.
Client:urgent — I need a home move tomorrow
AI:Hello! We take urgent orders. What are we moving, and do you need furniture disassembly? 🙂
Client:a sofa and two armchairs, no disassembly. 1st floor no elevator, 3rd floor with elevator
AI:Got it! A van + 2 movers, roughly $80–100 for 2 hours. What time tomorrow works? 8:00, 10:00 and 14:00 are open. 🙂
Client:10 works
AI:Booked for tomorrow at 10:00. Leave your name and phone — I'll confirm the order and send the vehicle details. 🙂
Result: the bot took the urgent order, collected the parameters (cargo, floors, elevator, time), quoted a price range and closed with contacts — no extra back-and-forth.

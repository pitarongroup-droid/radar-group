Cases

How AI Manager is trained for your product

Six examples. One mechanic: we study the product, audience and objections, then train the bot — and it sells like your best salesperson.

01

Auto service

What we studied: car models, typical jobs, price ranges, timelines, warranty on work and parts. Frequent questions: "How much is an oil change?", "How long does it take?", "Do you give a warranty?"
Client:how much is an oil change for a Camry?
AI:For a 2019 Camry — $120 including labor and filter if you use our oil. With your own — $40. What oil do you use? 🙂
Client:my own, Mobil
AI:Great, Mobil 1 works. Then $40 + filter $15. Want to book? 🙂
Client:do you give a warranty?
AI:Yes, on the work — one year. If anything goes wrong, we redo it for free. Book for Saturday? 🙂
Client:ok
AI:Perfect! Leave your number — the mechanic will confirm the time 🙂
Result: the bot knows prices, warranty, answers "expensive" and books with full parameters.
02

Online school / Courses

What we studied: course programs, formats, duration, prices, installments, graduate reviews. Objections: "expensive", "let me think", "no time to study".
Client:I want to learn Python, how much?
AI:The course is 8 weeks, $300 or 4 payments of $80. The first trial lesson is free. Are you starting from scratch or already coding? 🙂
Client:from scratch. but it's expensive for me
AI:I get it 🙂 That's why we have installments — $80, like 2-3 coffees a week. And you can try the first 3 lessons before paying. Book a free trial? 🙂
Client:ok
AI:Great! Leave your number — the curator will reach out and pick a convenient time 🙂
Result: "expensive" objection handled with installments and value, not a discount.
03

Dental clinic

What we studied: services, prices, equipment, doctors, contraindications. Objections: "I'm scared", "expensive", "can it be cheaper".
Client:severe toothache, can I come today?
AI:I'm sorry 🙁 Upper or lower tooth? Sharp or dull pain? 🙂
Client:lower right, sharp
AI:Sounds like pulpitis. We have an urgent slot today at 6:30 PM and tomorrow at 10 AM. Exam with diagnostics — $25, treatment — from $80. Which works? 🙂
Client:expensive, can it be cheaper?
AI:I understand 🙂 If you wait, it can turn into extraction — that's already from $200. Right now we can fix it with treatment. Shall I book you for 6:30? 🙂
Client:ok, book it
Result: the bot answers "expensive" through value and urgency, not a discount.
04

Legal services

What we studied: case categories, court practice, timelines, fees. Objections: "what if we lose?", "how much will it cost?", "let me think".
Client:my employer hasn't paid salary for 3 months, what should I do?
AI:This is solvable. Are you officially employed? Do you have a contract, orders? 🙂
Client:officially, I have a contract
AI:Great. Then you can claim not only the debt, but also compensation for the delay. In practice, 85% of such cases are won. Consultation — $30, then we work on results. Leave a request? 🙂
Client:what if we lose?
AI:I understand your concern 🙂 If you have the contract and messages — that's enough evidence. Plus we can first assess the chances during a consultation, without paying for the case. Book you in? 🙂
Result: "what if" objection closed through numbers and a safe first step.
05

Beauty salon / Barbershop

What we studied: price list, stylists, service duration, calendar slots. Objections: "expensive", "who is the stylist?", "can it be earlier".
Client:how much is a haircut + beard?
AI:Haircut $25, beard $15. Together $35 — you save $5. We have slots today at 7 PM and 8 PM. Which suits you? 🙂
Client:who are the stylists?
AI:At 7 PM — Alexey (10 years of experience, specializes in fades), at 8 PM — Dmitry (beard and classic). Who do you prefer? 🙂
Client:Alexey, but I can't make 7
AI:Alexey has tomorrow at 6 PM and 8:30 PM. Works? 🙂
Result: the bot knows stylists and availability — and doesn't lose a client over a single "I can't make it".
06

Apartment renovation

What we studied: renovation types, square meters, price per m², timelines, warranty. Objections: "can it be cheaper?", "who will do it?", "what if something goes wrong?"
Client:how much is renovation for a 60 sqm two-bedroom?
AI:Cosmetic — from $3000, with replanning — from $5500. Timeline: 3-5 weeks. What exactly do you need? 🙂
Client:cosmetic. can it be cheaper?
AI:I understand 🙂 $3000 is turnkey, with materials and a 2-year warranty. If you do it without warranty with freelancers — about 30% cheaper, but if something goes wrong, you redo it at your own expense. Shall I book a measurement? The foreman will come and give an exact estimate 🙂
Client:ok
Result: the bot defends the price through warranty and risks, doesn't give in for free.

About the technology

Why it's not "just a bot"

An ordinary bot is a menu with buttons. It serves, but doesn't sell. The AI Manager is a trained salesperson who knows your product, understands the client and leads them to a deal.

Learns your product

We upload your catalog, specs, prices, UVP, differences from competitors. The bot answers like an expert.

Handles objections

"Expensive", "let me think", "competitors are cheaper", "not now" — we craft specific responses for each niche.

Remembers the whole dialogue

Doesn't ask the same question twice. Takes into account everything the client said earlier in the conversation.

4 follow-up stages

Follows up in 15 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day, 2 days. Gently, without pressure, respecting quiet hours.

Quality control

Cleans responses, never invents anything not in your product. Adds a question if the dialogue stalls.

Delivers hot leads

You get a lead with contact, task, handled objections and full dialogue log.

Honestly

When AI Manager won't fit

It doesn't replace complex B2B sales with long cycles, custom terms and top-level negotiations. But it's ideal for typical services, first-line qualification and unloading your managers. For complex deals — it prepares the ground, and the final close stays with a human.

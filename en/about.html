Methodology · Product-specific training

Not just a chatbot.
A sales manager trained for your business.

We don't launch an AI with generic answers and hope for the best. We study what you sell, how your customers choose, and what stops them from buying — then build and test a practical conversation flow around your business.

The difference

A generic bot answers. A trained AI Manager helps move the sale forward.

The goal is not to chat for the sake of chatting. The goal is to understand the request, provide relevant information and guide a potential customer toward the next useful step.

Knows your product

Uses your product information, service descriptions, pricing rules and business-specific details.

Follows a conversation logic

Asks clarifying questions, responds in context and avoids treating every inquiry as the same.

Handles objections

Addresses concerns about price, timing or suitability using the information and rules agreed with you.

Captures useful details

Collects contact information and the context a human manager needs to follow up.

Our process

From business knowledge to a tested assistant

The setup is tailored to your niche and the way your team works.

01

Study the business

We collect product details, services, pricing, audience questions and the boundaries the assistant must respect.

02

Map customer intent

We identify common requests, decision factors, objections and the information needed to qualify a lead.

03

Build the dialogue

We define tone of voice, clarifying questions, response principles, lead handoff and escalation cases.

04

Test real scenarios

We check typical and difficult conversations, improve weak responses and prepare the assistant for launch.

Quality control

What we check before launch

Product accuracy. Answers should reflect the information you provide and avoid invented prices or promises.
Useful questions. The assistant should clarify the customer's request without turning the conversation into an interrogation.
Clear next steps. When a person is ready to proceed, the assistant should collect the right details or pass the conversation to your team.
Safe boundaries. If the assistant lacks reliable information or the request needs a human, it should not pretend otherwise.
Where it works

A car service, an online school and a professional service have different customer questions. Training starts with those differences.
